### 2026-07-28T13:51:00-04:00: Archive gate measures archivable bytes and reports total size
**By:** Rusty
**What:** Scribe's decision archive gate now measures `archivable_bytes`, not whole-file bytes, for archive triggers. `archivable_bytes` is the UTF-8 byte sum of every top-level `## ` section except exact `## Standing Policy`, including each counted section header through the byte before the next top-level section; the file title/preamble are excluded and missing sections count as 0. `total_bytes` remains in the HEALTH REPORT as reporting-only context-cost visibility and must never trigger a sweep. The raised archive tiers are 24 KiB (30-day non-durable sweep) and 64 KiB (7-day non-durable sweep). Standing Policy remains unarchivable; durable entries are retained/promoted, and pressure is reported rather than evicting durable content. This supersedes the threshold and measured-denominator half of Rusty's earlier `b288603` / `2026-07-28: Archive gate requires age AND durability` decision; that decision's durability test and Standing Policy never-archive rule remain binding.
**Why:** The previous whole-file ceiling measured the structurally unarchivable Standing Policy corpus, so the gate became permanently red once durable policy alone exceeded the old budget. Today's file is 21,138 total bytes but only 3,481 archivable bytes, so the correct action is no archive and no pressure while still surfacing total size for future durable-corpus tightening. The 24 KiB Tier 1 gives substantial headroom over the current ~3.4 KiB archivable working set without hiding multi-sprint bloat; the 64 KiB Tier 2 is an upper emergency budget before the stricter 7-day cleanup applies.
